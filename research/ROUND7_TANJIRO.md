## 3. MASTER PROMPT compliance (coding workflow)

- [x] Script-only narration, hook→setting→escalation→pivot→twist, ~3.7 w/s target.
- [x] Timing measured from the Piper waveform (`align.py` energy islands) **before**
      the shot plan; no words-per-second guess.
- [x] No shot > 90 frames (3 s); scene changes every ≤3 s (24 shots / 55 s).
- [x] Camera angle never repeats consecutively (`qaShotPlan` enforces; 0 problems).
- [x] Every shot has a camera move; every hold creeps; smootherstep glides;
      3–5° roll on dramatic beats; impact shake on slash/boulder/wheel.
- [x] Twist line ("Then the dragon rises. Water Wheel!") = music stop + low drone +
      sting + push-in with roll.
- [x] Characters face movement direction; walk phase drives legs (no gliding);
      idle breathing + blink on every character every frame.
- [x] Stickman style: pure-white imperfect heads, thick navy ink, black stick limbs,
      dot hands, no shoes, no nose, no face shading, outfit = body blob.
- [x] Illustrated worlds with ink outlines, painted textures, atmospheric haze.
- [x] Music sits under voice (ducked ~6 dB); SFX peak capped at 0.7× voice peak
      (measured: A gain 0.46, B gain auto).
- [x] Finish −14 LUFS target / −1 dBTP limiter; H.264 + AAC.
- [x] QA: no frozen spans ≥0.4 s, no black frames (both PASS).
- [x] Captions (white bold lowercase, soft shadow, word-grouped from measured timing).
- [x] 3 thumbnails + title/description/tags (`exp7a-metadata.json`).

## 4. Defects found by looking / measuring, and fixed

1. **Stickman drawn below ground.** Unit space has y=1 at the feet but the origin
   wasn't offset, so figures rendered a full height too low (heads at the bottom of
   frame). Fixed with `translate(0,-1)` after the scale. Found in the first contact sheet.
2. **PNG encode dominated frame cost (805 ms/frame).** Switched intermediate frames
   to JPEG q92 (we re-encode to H.264 anyway): 842 → 159 ms/frame, a 5.3× speedup.
3. **`plan7.lineOffsets` returned the post-increment offset**, shifting every line's
   word span and crashing the planner. Fixed to return the captured offset.
4. **3D night wide shot was an empty frame** — character too far/dark to read.
   Tightened the camera (7.5→5.0 units) and raised night key/rim/exposure.
5. **3D freeze ≥0.4 s at the river shot start (2.33–2.77 s).** A pure ease-in-out
   camera move has zero initial velocity. Fixed by blending a linear creep
   (`0.28k + 0.72·ease`) plus always-on micro handheld drift.
6. **3D boulder shot was a gray wall** (camera too close, no visible split). Pulled
   the camera back and rebuilt the boulder as two halves that separate and tilt as
   the cut lands, plus a falling cap stone — the split now reads.
7. **3D freezes persisted at wide-shot opens after #5** (river 2.33 s, boulder
   9.47 s): a distant small subject + slow move compresses to near-identical frames
   at CRF 17. Fixed with a linear zoom creep active from frame 0 (`fov·(1−0.06k)`),
   stronger handheld drift, a closer river camera, an earlier boulder split, and grain
   raised 0.05→0.07 so flat sky/snow survives compression (the 2D passed for the same
   reason). Re-rendered; **0 black, 0 freezes — PASS.**

## 5. Honest limitations (not papered over)

- **I cannot hear the audio.** All audio claims are measurements (LUFS, peak, SFX
  cap, stream presence). The score is procedural, not a composed/licensed track.
- **Motion was judged from sampled frames / contact sheets**, not continuous viewing.
- The 2D stickman is on-style but **not** the Round 6 implicit-body fidelity; it is a
  deliberate flat-marker look per the MASTER PROMPT. The 3D figure is the Round 5
  low-poly rig (faceted, mitten hands), not the Round 6 smooth body.
- The water dragon is a **stylised 2D composite** over the 3D render in Experiment B
  (screen-space overlay), not a simulated 3D fluid. This is the same 2.5D approach
  Round 5 used for snow/shafts and is appropriate to the anime look.
- Lip-sync in these two shorts is caption-level; the 3D figure's mouth opens on
  action beats but is not phoneme-driven here (the Round 5/6 viseme path exists and
  can be wired in next).
- The script is a **placeholder** pending the user's original Tanjiro script.
- LUFS landed at −15.4 (A) / −14.9 (B) vs the −14 target — within ~1.4 dB; YouTube
  normalises to −14 on playback. Not a blocker, noted for honesty.
