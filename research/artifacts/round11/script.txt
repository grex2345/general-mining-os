Everyone in the village laughed at Sami. While the others chased gold, he chose clay. They called him crazy. They called him slow. But every morning, his hands were already spinning. He shaped a thousand pots, and threw a thousand more away. Then one night, he fired the kiln hotter than ever before. When the doors swung open, the whole village fell silent. Because what came out wasn't just pottery. It was something no one had ever seen. And the boy they laughed at became the one they remembered.
