For sixty years, the bell of Vellara had never made a sound. Every founder in the valley tried. They poured bronze, they tuned the crown, they hung it higher. And every time, the tower stayed silent. Then a girl named Nora climbed the stairs with a hammer no bigger than her hand. She did not touch the bell. She tapped the wall beside it, once, twice, listening. The whole town held its breath. Because the bell was never broken. The tower was. Something in that stone had been swallowing every note for sixty years. She moved the bell four feet to the left. And when it rang, they heard it in three villages. The girl they ignored became the one they listened to.
