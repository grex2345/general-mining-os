In Jujutsu Kaisen, Gojo's infinity isn't a shield; it's a coffin, and he's sealed inside it. Infinity stops anything from touching him, but if nothing gets in, nothing gets out. Infinity isn't a wall; it's a thermos. NASA spent millions solving this exact problem. And astronauts' suit is a perfect shield, and it still needs a radiator. You generate about 100 watts of heat doing nothing. A high-speed fight, roughly a thousand. Sweating can't save Gojo. The sweat can't evaporate. Roughly four minutes per degree, twenty minutes, and his brain starts cooking. He bakes inside his own defense. That's why the six eyes are mandatory. He isn't just blocking things; he manually filters every single atom: oxygen in, heat out, knives out. If he loses focus for one second, he cooks. He runs reverse curse technique 24/7 just to heal the brain damage of doing thermal calculus. NASA built a radiator. Gojo built a brain that never switches off. That's the difference. One can be switched off for maintenance; the other can't. You've got your own infinity, a rule you never break, so nothing hurts you. The call you don't make, the thing you never try. Nothing gets in, nothing gets out. So what's yours?
