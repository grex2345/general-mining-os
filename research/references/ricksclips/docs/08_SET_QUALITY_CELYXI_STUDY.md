# Set quality bar: Celyxi, "The Outlaw Who Became The Sheriff" (68 s), studied frame by frame twice

**THE SETS SHOULD BE AS DETAILED AS POSSIBLE. DO NOT SLACK OFF THE SETS. THE SETS ARE THE MAIN ATTRACTION.**
Our characters are better than Celyxi's. Their sets, and their fox character, are the bar. Contact sheets at 2 fps plus full-res detail frames are in `reference/celyxi/`.

## Pass 1: what is on screen (0-68 s)
- **0.0 s.** Aerial establishing shot down a full western main street. About 30 buildings on both sides, all with false fronts, balconies, striped awnings, signs (ARBUCKLES' COFFEE), boardwalks, hitching rails and barrels. A covered wagon, horses and a crowd sit in the middle. A water tower, windmill and church sit in the hazy distance. The sky has painted clouds.
- **1-5 s.** A crowd two-shot, then a slide to a wall of real WANTED posters: aged paper, a star rating, the reward, small print and a rider name. It ends on a poster macro.
- **6-8 s.** A saloon porch with chickens, a gunfight with a pig, then a red two-storey bank. The bank has white window trim with mullions, a panelled door, a barrel, a hitching post, chickens and a robber with a money bag.
- **8-9 s.** A Boot Hill graveyard: crosses with sheriff stars and hats, dirt mounds with pebbles, skulls, a cactus, a picket fence, a dead tree with a vulture, and a windmill behind.
- **10-17 s.** A street dialogue two-shot with the town blurred behind (deep DOF). A close-up as he pins on the star, with a glint.
- **18-25 s.** A saloon card table with a suitcase and cards, a horse scene, then the gunman's revolver pointing at the lens.
- **25-30 s.** City Hall: brick, a clock, bunting, band members on the balcony, a gold rifle presented with an engraved plaque, "CALDWELL · KANSAS" lettering.
- **30-31 s.** A wedding at a white house with a picket fence and sunflowers.
- **32-38 s.** **Weather and time change.** A rainy night ride with streaks of rain, wet green fields, a fence and telegraph poles from above.
- **38-47 s.** The bank robbery in rain-light: a teller cage with bars, a green visor, a tiled floor, a huge round vault door with a wheel and dial, deposit-box walls.
- **47-49 s.** **Sunset:** a red canyon, a posse on horses, everything tinted orange.
- **49-51 s.** A night lynch mob with lanterns, the JAIL sign, a moon.
- **52-57 s.** A museum: a spotlit glass case, the gold rifle, a "KANSAS HISTORICAL SOCIETY" plaque, a sepia portrait, WANTED frames, a velvet rope, dust in the light beam.
- **58-63 s.** The sheriff's office with WANTED wall posters, then a hideout cave with glowing eyes.
- **63-68 s.** **The fox analogy:** a red barn with "NO FOXES!", a chicken in the window, sunflowers, a chicken-feed bag, a tipped bucket, chicks, a hen. The fox wears the sheriff's coat and star, with a stitched scar, a smirk and a big bushy tail.

## Pass 2: why the sets read as expensive (what we must match)
1. **Real architecture, not boxes.** Window frames with trim and mullions, clapboard and brick textures, false fronts, balconies, porches, panelled doors, steps, roof overhangs. Every building has 6-10 parts.
2. **Ground is never flat colour.** Dirt with wagon-wheel ruts, grass tufts, pebbles, skulls, hay, puddles. Painted texture plus 3D clutter on top.
3. **Clutter everywhere:** barrels, crates, hitching rails, buckets, sacks, chickens, posters layered on posters, bunting, lanterns. No empty floor or wall anywhere in frame.
4. **Animals and life:** chickens, a pig, horses, a vulture, flying birds. Something alive in every set.
5. **Atmosphere:**
   - aerial haze and fog that fade the distance
   - a warm low sun with long soft shadows
   - ambient occlusion in the corners
   - heavy depth of field on dialogue (the background is a soft painting)
   - dust motes in light beams
6. **Light changes with the story:** day → rain night → sunset red → moonlit night → warm museum. Characters take the scene's tint (blue in rain, orange at sunset).
7. **Deep backgrounds:** every exterior shows layers (near street, mid buildings, far town, horizon, sky with clouds). Every interior has a window or doorway showing more world.
8. **Hero props get the macro treatment:** WANTED posters with full fake print, a gold rifle with an engraved plaque, a star with a glint, a vault wheel. Each is held 1-2 s with a slow push.
9. **The analogy becomes a character:** "like hiring a fox to guard the henhouse" is shown literally, with a fully designed anthropomorphic fox in its own dressed set. Do this for every "You see / it's like…" line.
10. **Typography in the world:** shop signs, plaques and posters in period fonts, aged and weathered. Never a flat sign with clean text on a coloured rectangle.

## Typical ways a Blender set falls short
Flat untextured floors and walls; boxes for buildings; signs as clean rectangles; empty walls; no haze; no animals; no light or weather changes; no DOF; one lighting setup for everything.
