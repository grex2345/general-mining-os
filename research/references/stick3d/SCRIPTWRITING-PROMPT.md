# 3D Stick Figure Master — Scriptwriting Prompt (from the user's Drive .docx)

Source: "3D stick figure master prompt.docx" (Google Drive, plugin folder). This is the
STORYTELLING/SCRIPT half of the skill; the production half is Stick3D-Pack-v4 (see MASTER-PROMPT.md).

## STAGE 1 — Training + niche selection
Expert YouTube Shorts scriptwriter, ultra-tight micro-story. Cinematic, punchy, story-driven.
Every script opens mid-action with zero warm-up, uses one tight narrative to explain a real
phenomenon / psychological principle / historical event / bizarre fact, and ends the moment the
concept lands. Five niche options + custom:
1. Bizarre real-world phenomena (target fixation, Baader-Meinhof effect).
2. What-if history went differently (Library of Alexandria never burned).
3. Smartest scams/hustles in history (salting a mine; the man who sold the Eiffel Tower twice).
4. Accidental discoveries (safety glass, the Slinky).
5. Ancient weapons / war tricks that worked (flaming-horn cattle stampede).
6. Custom niche.

## STAGE 2 — Topic generation
20 unique topics for the chosen niche. Each: punchy specific title, core hook (the bizarre
fact/event/concept), story angle (the specific character or moment the story opens on).

## STAGE 3 — Research (if needed)
Real events/people/science: internal fact-check (names, dates, sequence, mechanism); flag
uncertainty before writing. Fictional/hypothetical: skip research, go straight to script.

## STAGE 4 — Script writing
CONTENT: 800–900 characters only (count carefully). Open mid-action (no intro/setup fluff).
Story-driven (concept explains itself through what happens). Every line pulls forward (no filler).
End the moment the concept lands (no summary, no "so that's why", no call to action). Name the
real phenomenon naturally inside the story (not textbook).
FORMAT: plain flowing prose only; no headings/bullets/labels/scene directions/character tags.
After writing, show the exact character count.

## Adaptation for our engine + Moroccan Darija
- Keep the structure (hook → myth/question → story/science → payoff) but write the narration in
  natural Darija; count characters loosely (Darija Arabic script differs); end on the payoff.
- The 800–900 char target ≈ 45–60 s at a natural pace; longer stories (e.g. kounkan's 2:50) scale up.
